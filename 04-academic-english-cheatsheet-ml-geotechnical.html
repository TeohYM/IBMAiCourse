ML & Geotechnical Engineering

📰 原文摘要 Abstract
🌐 English (學習語料)
Soil liquefaction has long been a critical issue in geotechnical engineering. Among the laboratory methods developed to investigate this phenomenon, the cyclic triaxial test is one of the most important experiments for simulating soil behavior under earthquake loading. A key parameter in this test is the Cyclic Stress Ratio (CSR), which reflects the magnitude of loading energy applied to the soil specimen. However, the appropriate CSR value often depends on the specimen's physical properties, the experimental conditions, and the experience of the test operator.

To address the problem, this study collected nearly 1,000 cyclic triaxial test records from 23 academic papers and established multiple machine learning models to predict CSR. The study is divided into three main parts: data preprocessing and feature engineering (including missing value imputation); model development using seven supervised models; and binary classification with model interpretability and prediction uncertainty analysis. The results show that CatBoost, XGBoost, and Random Forest achieved the best overall performance. MICE produced the most stable imputation outcomes. EBM provided interpretable visualizations, while BNN offered prediction intervals that helped quantify uncertainty.
🇹🇼 中文原文摘要
土壤液化一直是大地工程領域長期關注的重要議題。動態三軸試驗為模擬地震載重下研究土壤行為的主要試驗，其中循環剪應力比（CSR）的設定往往依賴試體的物理性質、試驗條件和試驗者的經驗。本研究蒐集近千筆試驗資料，建立多種機器學習模型協助預測 CSR。結果顯示 CatBoost、XGBoost 與 Random Forest 整體表現最佳；MICE 為最穩定補值方式；EBM 提供可解釋性，BNN 量化預測不確定性。

🔍 文法句型剖析 Grammar Patterns (3 patterns)
① However, S + often depends on + [A, B, and C]　— 學術轉折 + Oxford Comma
However, the appropriate CSR value often depends on the specimen's physical properties, the experimental conditions, and the experience of the test operator.
📌 However 置句首作轉折，後加逗號。depend on 為不可分動詞片語。三元並列用 Oxford comma（最後 and 前加逗號），是學術英文標準。
✏️ However, the optimal learning rate often depends on the dataset size, the model architecture, and the chosen optimizer.
然而，最佳學習率往往取決於資料集大小、模型架構以及優化演算法。
⚠️ 口語用 But；學術寫作用 However / Nevertheless / Nonetheless
② The study is divided into N parts. The first part involves…, including…　— 論文結構說明
The study is divided into three main parts. The first part involves data preprocessing and feature engineering, including missing value imputation, numerical variable standardization, and categorical variable encoding.
📌 be divided into 為學術摘要常用被動式，強調結構而非執行者。including 引導補充例子，前加逗號。
✏️ The experiment is divided into four stages. The first stage involves sample preparation, including mixing, compaction, and saturation.
實驗分為四個階段。第一階段涉及試樣製備，包括混合、壓實和飽和。
⚠️ 口語可說 split into / broken down into；正式學術偏用 divided into
③ The results show that S + achieved… Moreover, S + were able to…　— 結果報告 + 遞進
The results show that CatBoost, XGBoost, and Random Forest achieved the best overall performance. Moreover, the models were able to make accurate predictions using only a set of primary features.
📌 show that 引導 that 子句報告核心發現。Moreover / Furthermore / In addition 置句首加逗號表遞進。using only 為分詞片語，強調「僅需」。
✏️ The results indicate that the proposed model achieved the highest accuracy. Furthermore, it classified unseen samples using only three features.
結果顯示所提出的模型達到最高精度。此外，僅用三個特徵即可分類未見樣本。
⚠️ 學術寫作偏用 show / indicate / suggest，避免 prove（過於強烈）

⚠️ 中英翻譯陷阱 Translation Traps
中文「預測」→ predict（具體數值）/ estimate（估算）/ forecast（未來趨勢）—— 本論文用 predict（迴歸預測數值）
「決定係數」→ 正式寫 coefficient of determination，口頭說 R-squared
「表現最佳」→ 學術寫 achieved the best overall performance 或 outperformed，避免 "is the best"（過於口語）
「學術語氣」→ 用 show / indicate / suggest，不用 prove（主張太強）
